### Your job search best friend.

I built Job View to help me find roles and figure out how to position myself for them. A career counsellor helps me connect my experience to specific openings. A Swiss job market specialist draws on public resources, including ETH Zürich’s Career Center guidance.

#### From a shortlist to a draft

Three agents prepare a positioning brief, draft the CV and cover letter, and review the claims. After a draft misattributed finite-element modelling to one of my papers, I added a check against the publications. I decide what to send.
